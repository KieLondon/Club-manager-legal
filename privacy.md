# Club Manager — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 16 August 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how **Club Manager** collects, uses, stores and protects information when you use the Club Manager Discord application, bot and associated services.

Club Manager is operated by **Kie London**, trading as **Club Manager**.

For the purposes of applicable UK data protection law, Kie London trading as Club Manager is responsible for determining how personal information processed directly by Club Manager is used.

If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, you should not use Club Manager.

## 1. About Club Manager

Club Manager is a Discord application designed primarily for EA SPORTS FC Pro Clubs, esports clubs and gaming communities.

The Service may provide features including:

* Team and player management
* Player availability
* Matchday management
* Line-up creation
* Club graphics
* Player signing graphics
* MVP and player awards
* Polls and voting
* Recruitment
* Match statistics
* Player statistics
* Club analytics
* Automated reports
* Tournament functionality
* Matchday reminders
* Lobby management
* Server-specific configuration
* Custom club branding
* Other related club-management features

To provide these features, Club Manager may need to process limited information relating to Discord servers, Discord users, clubs, players and matches.

## 2. Information Club Manager May Collect

The exact information processed depends on which Club Manager features are used.

### Discord account information

Club Manager may receive or process information made available through Discord, including:

* Discord user ID
* Discord username
* Discord display name
* Discord avatar
* Server nickname
* Discord roles
* Server membership information where required for a feature
* User interactions with Club Manager commands or buttons

A Discord user ID is a unique numerical identifier provided by Discord.

Club Manager may use Discord IDs rather than names wherever practical because usernames and display names can change.

## 3. Discord Server Information

* Feature settings

## 6. EA SPORTS FC and Match Information

Some Club Manager features may obtain or process information relating to EA SPORTS FC and Pro Clubs.

This may include:

* Club identifiers
* Club names

* Match dates and times
* Match results
* Goals
* Player statistics
* Player ratings
* Match performance information
* Other information associated with a club or match

This information may be obtained from Electronic Arts services, interfaces or other legitimate sources available to Club Manager.

Club Manager may combine match information with information supplied by a Discord server in order to produce statistics, reports and analytics.

Club Manager is not affiliated with or endorsed by Electronic Arts Inc. or EA SPORTS.

## 7. Commands and Message Content

Club Manager processes information submitted directly through commands, buttons, forms and other interactions where this is necessary to perform the requested feature.

Club Manager is **not designed to collect or permanently store general Discord conversations**.

Normal conversation within a Discord server is not intentionally collected simply because Club Manager is present in that server.

However, information deliberately submitted to a Club Manager command or feature may be processed where required to provide that feature.

For example, if a manager enters a player's name into a Club Manager command, the player name may be processed and stored as part of that feature.

Club Manager does not use Discord message content to train artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.

## 16. Who Information May Be Shared With

Club Manager does **not sell personal information**.

Information may be shared only where reasonably necessary, including with:

Discord independently processes information in accordance with its own terms and privacy practices.

### Members of your Discord server

Information submitted to features intended to produce server-visible results may be displayed to relevant members of the same Discord server.

For example:

* A line-up may display player names
* Availability may be shown to managers
* An MVP graphic may display a player
* Analytics may display player statistics

The visibility of information depends on the feature and Discord permissions used by the server.

### Service providers

Limited information may be processed by technology, hosting, storage, payment or support providers where reasonably necessary to operate Club Manager.

Any provider acting on behalf of Club Manager is expected to handle information appropriately and only for the relevant service.

### Legal requirements

Information may be disclosed where reasonably necessary to:

* Comply with applicable law

## 20. How Long Information Is Kept

Club Manager does not keep personal information indefinitely unless there is a legitimate reason to do so.

Retention depends on the type of information and why it is required.

Club and server information may generally be retained while:

* Club Manager is actively being used by the server;
* the relevant feature remains enabled;
* an active subscription exists;
* the information remains necessary to provide requested functionality; or
* retention is reasonably necessary for support, security or operational purposes.

When a server permanently stops using Club Manager, information may be retained for a reasonable period where necessary to resolve support issues, allow legitimate restoration, maintain financial records or comply with legal obligations.

Information that is no longer reasonably required will be deleted, anonymised or allowed to expire as appropriate.

Diagnostic and security information will generally be kept only for as long as reasonably necessary to investigate technical or security issues.

Financial and transaction records may be retained for the period required by applicable tax, accounting or legal requirements.

Backups may temporarily contain information after deletion from active systems until those backups are overwritten through the normal backup cycle.

## 21. Removing Club Manager

A Discord server owner or authorised administrator can remove Club Manager from their server using Discord's application-management functionality.

Removing the bot stops Club Manager from continuing to operate within that server.

Removal does not necessarily cause every historical record to disappear immediately where information must reasonably be retained for legal, security, subscription, backup or support purposes.

Server owners may contact Club Manager to request deletion of eligible server information.
